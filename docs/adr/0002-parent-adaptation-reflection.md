# 0002: test parent adaptation without a relationship grade

13 October 2026. Status: proposed experiment. Extends 0001 without editing or adopting its launch decision.

## Context

Miguel asks whether understanding the child can help the parent adapt how they show up. A therapeutic-alliance analogy and possible alliance score were proposed for assessment. Supplied research distinguishes therapy alliance, co-parent alliance and parent-child relationship constructs; no chat-derived universal composite is substantiated. Mango Parent, GiGi and Hatley occupy substantial parts of parent adaptation and continuity. Prior MVP code already holds parent context but silently accumulates it and couples it into child-agent synthesis.

## Proposed decision

Test a parent-controlled reflection/practice flow with observable event, adult interpretation/capacity, alternatives, one parent adjustment, private rehearsal and source-attributed follow-up. Preserve uncertainty and child agency; no numeric/composite score, compatibility type, diagnostic inference or caregiver ranking. Keep parent-private information separate from child/external context. Retain iteration 1 unchanged except the root index pointing to the current recommendation.

## Consequences

A scripted synthetic prototype can test the interaction before real-data infrastructure or research. It cannot validate a relationship claim. Full profile and dedicated app must earn value against simpler summaries and competent alternatives. A future instrument is a separate measurement-development and licensing project. Rights, provider/data controls, safety and approved research remain launch gates. No production changes, uploads, deployment or child-data processing are authorized by this ADR.
