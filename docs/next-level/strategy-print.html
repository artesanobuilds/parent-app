Buy evidence next.
Let the profile earn its complexity.

StageTest / decision
1 · Problem12–15 adult interviews with synthetic concepts. Concrete need, trust and device reality; no leading neuroscience pitch.
2 · Synthetic qualityAt least 30 varied benchmark cases; expand to 100 multi-session histories. Expert review of extraction, contradictions, sibling identity and forgetting.
3 · AblationSame model/content/history: generic memory vs reviewed summary vs full profile. Blind order; separate warmth from supported usefulness.
4 · Approved pilotOnly after rights, provider and privacy gates. Four weeks; voluntary use, useful learning and actual paid renewal—not chat volume.
5 · PortabilityExact-fields/destination comprehension plus time/result advantage over copying a simple note. Add read-only connector only if worthwhile.

Proposed business thresholds: roughly two-thirds blinded usefulness preference with incremental gain over a summary; no severe failures; at least half voluntarily use in three of four weeks and five renew a paid month in a 15–20-family discovery cohort. A 40–60-household comparative feasibility study is a later alternative—not a clinical efficacy trial. Predeclare one design; do not mix source proposals after seeing results.

Stop or reshape

Stop the profile thesis if generic memory ties, useful results require hidden inference, correction fails or worry increases. Episodic value may suit a bounded service or toolkit instead of subscription software.
