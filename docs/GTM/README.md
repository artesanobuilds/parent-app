# Go-To-Market (GTM)

The workspace for MVP's go-to-market strategy — positioning, audience, channels,
pricing, and launch. Product/engineering docs stay in `docs/` (see `roadmap.md`,
`system-architecture.md`); this folder is deliberately separate so GTM can move on
its own track.

## What's here

- **`MVP-Ground-Zero.docx`** — the foundational strategy / positioning document
  (brand, voice, audience, the "why now"). The starting point for everything below.
- **`ground-zero-critic-feedback.md`** — the Ground Zero doc pressure-tested through
  critical lenses (build discipline, etc.) to surface blind spots.
- **`ground-zero.py`** — generator that renders the Ground Zero `.docx` with the
  Hearth palette/voice (`python ground-zero.py`, needs `python-docx`).

## To build out (starter scaffold)

- **Positioning & messaging** — one-liner, category, the wedge vs. alternatives.
- **ICP / audience** — who the first users are, where they already are, their JTBD.
- **Channels & motion** — acquisition (organic/paid/community/referral), the beta→GA path.
- **Pricing & packaging** — tiers, what's free in the closed beta vs. paid.
- **Launch plan** — milestones, assets, the TestFlight→App Store timeline, metrics/goals.

> Current stage: **closed beta** (TestFlight, internal "POC" group). Real-world
> grounding + the product's differentiators live in the Ground Zero doc and the
> roadmap.
