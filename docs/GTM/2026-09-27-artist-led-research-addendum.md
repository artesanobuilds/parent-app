## Revise the research approach

1. **Understand lived experience.** Research specific difficult moments, what people already try, where support fails, and what helpful support feels like. Include quiet, overlooked needs. Treat interviews as listening and learning, not requests for people to design the product or approve my taste. Separate observed experience, published evidence, and conjecture.

2. **Offer creative seeds.** Describe three to five distinct experiences I might feel drawn to make. For each, show one concrete moment in a person's life, an example interaction, the change it might enable, and why my experience or interests could contribute. Include a direction beyond dads or separated parents. Do not force an overall market winner.

3. **Propose small creative experiments.** Adapt Rubin's seed → experiment → craft → finish heuristic. Suggest contrasting rough versions of promising experiences before settling their format. A conversation sketch, human-assisted session, or tiny practice tool may teach more than another competitive matrix. Describe what each experiment could reveal about the experience, usefulness, and my desire to keep making it. These are proposals, not authorization to build or recruit.

4. **Keep three judgments separate.** Creative conviction: does this feel honest, distinctive, and worth making to me? Human benefit: does it help people act, cope, connect, or develop agency in their lives? Sustainability: can I responsibly maintain it within my time and resources? Do not collapse them into a single weighted score. Enthusiasm does not establish benefit; low revenue does not establish lack of value.

5. **Reduce commercial research to proportionate context.** Keep a short section on practical costs, access, distribution, and ways to sustain the work. Explore small-scale, subsidized, partner-supported, or paid forms without assuming a venture-scale business. Earlier paid-pilot, conversion, and renewal thresholds are optional later business experiments, not gates that determine whether a creative seed deserves exploration. Do not invent my budget or assume unlimited time because I have a job.

6. **Preserve rigor around care.** Appraise clinical claims independently of my enthusiasm for a book or approach. Distinguish established findings, preliminary evidence, and personal philosophy. Keep consent, privacy, appropriate professional involvement, and the limits of AI support explicit. More engagement or protocol adherence is not automatically improved wellbeing. The aim is greater agency and real-world connection, not dependence on the coach.
