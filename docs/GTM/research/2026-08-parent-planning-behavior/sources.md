# Sources

Graded bibliography for the 2026-08 parent planning behavior study. Built 2026-08-22 from the
collector reports in `raw/` (P1 to P12, P5b, M1 to M5, S0 to S4), the verification reports (V1 to V7),
the chart datasets in `data/`, and the four VERIFIED CORRECTIONS blocks at the end of `BRIEF.md`.
The corrections blocks are authoritative and override any collector report.

## How to read this

Four tiers, applied as the brief defines them.

- **Tier A.** Government statistics or peer-reviewed research. US Census (ACS, CPS, FINC, FM, CH, FG
  series), BLS ATUS, NCES including NHES:PFI, NSCH, NHIS, NAMCS and NHAMCS, MEPS, CDC and NCHS, plus
  peer-reviewed journal articles (JAMA Network Open, American Sociological Review, Sex Roles, Journal
  of Marketing Research, Memory and Cognition, Pediatrics, American Family Physician, and the HCI
  literature). Statutes and regulations read directly are also Tier A.
- **Tier B.** Research organisations that publish their methodology: Pew Research Center, The Harris
  Poll, Gallup, Edison Research, Aspen Institute Project Play, RevenueCat, Ipsos KnowledgePanel,
  Afterschool Alliance, NORC AmeriSpeak, Common Sense Media, Menlo Ventures with Morning Consult.
- **Tier C.** Industry and vendor reports that disclose a method, plus this study's own original
  measurements. Vendor-interested sources are flagged explicitly in the entry. The Google Trends pulls,
  the ActivityHero index audit, the App Store review corpora, the Reddit verbatim corpus, the district
  calendar counts and the repository code audit are labelled original analyst measurement: reproducible
  method, no published n, no outside publisher asserting the finding.
- **Tier D.** SEO stat aggregators, listicles, "N statistics for 2026" pages. **Tier D is never
  citable and nothing in Tier D is listed here.**

This bibliography lists only what survived verification. Statistics the study refused to use are
catalogued separately in the report's kill-list appendix and in `data/killed_statistics.csv`. That
appendix carries a disclosed residual error rate of roughly 27 percent: across two re-tracing passes,
6 of 22 re-traced kills were overturned. Treat any single kill as about three in four reliable, not
certain, and read the per-item confidence and verifier attribution the appendix carries.

Verification status is mandatory on every entry below.

- **CONFIRMED.** A verifier reached the primary source and matched the figure.
- **PLAUSIBLE.** Provenance and arithmetic check out, but a declared assumption or an unre-fetched
  input sits inside it.
- **CORRECTED.** The collector's value, attribution, scope or framing was wrong. The correction is
  stated inline in the entry.
- **UNVERIFIED.** No verifier tested it. Cite only where the report visibly marks it as untested.

Two access facts a reader needs. `bls.gov` returns HTTP 403 to every direct fetch method in this
environment; every BLS figure was reached through Wayback Machine `id_` raw snapshots, which is the
standard BLS route for this project. CDC and NCHS figures were reached through the NIH/NCBI Bookshelf
and PMC mirrors of the identical government documents rather than `cdc.gov` directly.
