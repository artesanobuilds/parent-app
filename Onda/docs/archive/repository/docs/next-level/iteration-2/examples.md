# Parent adjustments: synthetic before and after

Original illustrative interactions, not Good Inside scripts or observed product outcomes. "After" means the adult changes an action; it does not guarantee a changed child, repaired bond or clinical benefit.

## A school morning

Before: "How do I make them stop refusing to cooperate?"

Observed: parent reports crying when shoes are requested at the doorway. An unfinished project is nearby. Parent reports rushing. Child perspective is unknown.

A useful question: "Could this be about leaving something unfinished, the shoes, the pace, or something else? What do we actually know?"

Parent adjustment: give one slower instruction, offer company or space, keep a necessary departure boundary while checking whether timing/expectations need revision. Do not presume all adult rules are justified. If pain or safety is involved, ordinary practice is not a substitute for appropriate help.

After: "I slowed down and used fewer words. They were still upset. I don't know yet what was hard for them."

Reflection: the changed adult response is reported; the child's feelings and relationship effect are not established. No failed-parent or low-alliance conclusion.

## Shared joy

Before: "What talent does this prove, and how do I develop it?"

Observed: parent reports their child making a cardboard world and inviting attention. Parent notices wanting to improve it.

Parent adjustment: ask which part the child wants to show; join as invited instead of evaluating or taking over. Let solo play remain a valid choice.

After: "I watched while they explained one room. I still don't know if explaining or making was the enjoyable part."

Reflection: a shared moment and uncertainty, not a giftedness score or fixed learning type.

## Repair

Before: "How do I get them to forgive me so we can move on?"

Observed: parent reports raising their voice and the child leaving quietly. Quiet does not establish forgiveness or safety.

Parent adjustment: acknowledge the adult's action, accept space or disagreement, make a modest change. Avoid asking for a hug, praise, forgiveness or reassurance.

After: "I owned raising my voice and offered to listen later. They did not want to talk."

Reflection: a reported repair attempt, not proof repair succeeded. Declining is not a trust rating. Repeated frightening conduct, abuse or immediate danger require human safety support beyond this concept.

## A constrained day

Before: "If I were more regulated, none of this would happen."

Observed: the parent reports little time, energy or support. These are constraints, not evidence of bad character or sole causation.

Parent adjustment: no new exercise today; simplify a nonessential demand or seek practical support if available. The app does not demand disclosure about poverty, health, disability or trauma to make advice smaller.

After: "I skipped the exercise. That tells us the plan may not fit my week."

Reflection: no streak loss or lower relationship grade. Parent needs count without making the child responsible for meeting adult emotional needs.
