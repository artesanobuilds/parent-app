# 0001 · Start Onda as a new product

Date: 2026-10-27. Status: accepted product direction from Miguel's instruction.

## Context

MVP explored parenting coaching, planning and family logistics. Next Level explored reviewed child understanding and parent adaptation. Those experiments and their evidence limits should survive, without implying that MVP's live product is being renamed or that earlier hypotheses were validated.

## Decision

MVP / Most Valued Parent is deprecated for future development. Onda is a brand-new product under top-level `Onda/`, with documentation under `Onda/docs/`. Its promise is to help parents understand their child so they can change how they show up. Archive research with provenance while preserving original paths, Git history and BB threads. Rename the BB project to Onda; preserve the remote repository name and legacy runtime identity.

This supersedes the undecided pivot framing in the September handoff and the product-direction status of earlier exploration ADRs. Those records remain unchanged. Onda's new-product decision does not establish a launch or investment gate as passed.

## Consequences

Use a dedicated foundation branch based on research commit `8a875ff020ebde78f111eb85303ced37ae7a7840`; no default-branch merge or deployment is included. New work is scoped under Onda, while legacy code remains available for reference and a planned retirement. No actual store takedown, provider cancellation, data movement/deletion or user notices now. Shutdown requires inventory, decisions and separate execution authorization.

Naming recommendation research is pending receipt. Onda is the user-selected name, not a claim of trademark/domain availability. Visual concepts are separate explorations and will be imported later. Differentiation and efficacy remain hypotheses.
