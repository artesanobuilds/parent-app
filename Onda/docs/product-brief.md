# Product brief · 27 October 2026

## Promise and audience

Help me understand my child so I can change how I show up. Onda serves parents with a conversational way to learn from ordinary family situations: hard moments, shared joy, and repair. It is a new product, not a reskin of MVP or a commitment to reuse its backend.

## Experience

Notice an episode → reinterpret with curiosity → choose one adjustment the parent controls → rehearse privately → try → reflect. Repeated situations build a living, correctable understanding. Separate observation, adult interpretation, possible child perspective and uncertainty. Ask before remembering; make corrections visible and persistent. Attribute observations rather than presenting assumptions as facts about the child.

Parents can adjust timing, language, expectations, environment, listening or repair. Low capacity should make the next action smaller. A child's disagreement or continuing distress is not a failure score. Joy and interests matter alongside difficulty. Repair allows space and does not demand forgiveness or reassurance.

## Boundaries and hypotheses

The earlier child-understanding and parent-adaptation work informs the starting hypothesis. Differentiation, demand, retention, willingness to pay and relationship benefit remain unvalidated. Existing competitors already address parent reflection, regulation and repair. Test against a reviewed summary and a competent general assistant before claiming an advantage.

No diagnostic labels, attachment grades, composite relationship score, covert monitoring or child-compliance metric. Parent-private disclosures must be separate from any child or co-parent context. Sharing requires a minimal reviewed preview. Storage, consent, retention, deletion, safeguarding and provider eligibility need deliberate design before real-family use.

## Next evidence

Use synthetic moments first. Check whether parents can distinguish observations from assumptions, correct the understanding, choose a feasible adjustment, rehearse it and understand sharing boundaries. Compare matched histories and attention across simpler alternatives. Study burden, voluntary use and adverse pressure; improvement claims need independent evidence beyond parent satisfaction. Clinical or commercial claims and content rights remain unresolved.

## Naming readiness

Onda remains the chosen working name. The [received naming report](research/naming/README.md) recommends bilingual warmth and same-wavelength meaning, with Understory and Ajá as fallbacks. These are author judgments; no parent testing was conducted. Preliminary trademark conflicts require qualified clearance before public commitment. Bilingual meaning, pronunciation, recall, inclusion and trust require caregiver validation before launch.
