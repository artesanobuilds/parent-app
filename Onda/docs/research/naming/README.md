## Recommendation and limits

The report recommends **Onda** for warmth, shortness, bilingual reach and the Spanish same-wavelength idiom. It proposes “Get on their wavelength” / “En la misma onda” as taglines. These are research recommendations; the tagline and bilingual interpretations have not been validated with parents.

Onda remains Miguel's chosen **working name**. The report's 44/50 score is author judgment, not measured consumer preference. Its claimed relationship-language gap and competitor findings are report assertions, not independently verified differentiation. No real parent testing was conducted.

The report flags possible ONDA software conflicts, including a reported Onda Holding S.A. filing (serial 79383381), another reported Class 42 ONDA filing (98208228), and an ONDAS medical-software registration. Screening relied on search snippets rather than live registry records; statuses, ownership, goods and jurisdictions need verification. **Onda is not trademark-cleared.** Domain variants and most international registers were not checked. A logo or composite name does not establish clearance.

**Understory** is the proposed English-first backup; **Ajá** is another bilingual fallback. They also require clearance and parent validation. Mirada was not screened. Nothing here authorizes a second BB rename or a public name commitment.
