## Who we are

MVP is a parenting app built by a small founding team. It is currently a **closed beta**: invite
only, free, and used by families we know. The fastest way to reach us about anything in this
policy is **Settings → Report a problem** in the iPhone app — it goes straight to the person
building this.

## What we collect

**Your account.** When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name and email address
from that provider. We never see your password.

**Your family profile.** What you tell us during onboarding and in Settings: your name, ZIP code,
your kids' names, ages, and details like temperament and interests. The app also learns over time
from your conversations (for example, what helps your child calm down) — everything it learns is
visible and editable in Settings → Profile → "What we've learned."

**Coach conversations.** Your chats with the parenting coach are stored so the coach remembers
your family between conversations.

**Kid companion conversations.** If you set up a companion for your child (always your choice,
with an explicit consent step), your child's messages are stored — and **you can read every one of
them**. There is no hidden channel: safety concerns lock the conversation and notify you
immediately.

**Your family's plan.** Activities, camps, registrations, calendar entries, to-dos, caregivers,
and anything you ask the planner to track.

**Messages you send other parents.** MVP has a simple parent-to-parent messaging feature. Your
messages are stored so both of you can read the thread, and they're deleted when either of you
deletes your account (both sides of the thread go). You can report or block any conversation at
any time. So other parents can find you, **your display name is visible to other parents in the
beta** — in the community and in the message directory.

**What you save.** The Saved tab keeps the articles, community posts, and coach replies you
bookmark. We also use your saves as a signal of what you actually care about — the topics you
keep saving help your coach tailor its advice, alongside what you told us at onboarding.
Unsaving something is part of that signal too. Saves are deleted with your account like
everything else.

**Photos of flyers and emails you share.** When you snap a camp flyer or paste an email, we
extract the useful details (dates, prices, deadlines). The extracted details are kept; **the image
itself is not stored** after processing.

**Gmail and Google Calendar, only if you connect them.** Gmail access is read-only and scoped to
finding activity-related email (registrations, schedules); we never send email on your behalf.
Calendar sync is two-way so your family's plan and your calendar stay in step. You can disconnect
either at any time in Settings → Connections, which also revokes our access.

**Diagnostics and analytics.** If you tap "Send report," we receive app and device diagnostics
you can preview first — no chat content. We also collect basic product analytics (which screens
get used) under an opaque random identifier that is never your name or email, with no advertising
trackers and no session recording.

## What we do with it

We use your data to run the product for your family: personalize the coach, keep the plan
accurate, watch registration windows, and keep your child's companion safe. That's it.

- **We never sell your data.**
- **We never show ads and never share data with ad networks.**
- **We don't use your family's data to train AI models.**

## Who processes it for us

Running MVP relies on a small set of service providers, each receiving only what its job needs:

| Provider | Job |
| --- | --- |
| OpenAI | Powers the AI coach, planner, and companions (processes conversation text) |
| ElevenLabs | Powers voice conversations (processes audio) |
| Apple / Google | Sign-in |
| Google | Gmail and Calendar sync, only if you connect them |
| Tavily | Web search for finding activities near you (receives search queries, not your identity) |
| Railway | Hosts our servers and database |
| Sentry, Grafana Cloud | Error and performance monitoring |
| PostHog | Product analytics (opaque identifier only) |

Data is encrypted in transit everywhere. Connected-account credentials (like your Google tokens)
are encrypted at rest.

## Children's privacy

MVP's kid companion exists **only** behind a parent consent step, and the parent always has full
access to the transcript. Safety-concerning messages lock the conversation and notify the parent.
We do not market to children, and the closed beta is not a public offering. As we say in the app:
the closed beta operates under direct parental consent and is **not yet COPPA-compliant** for a
public launch — public release will require additional compliance work, which we are scheduling.

## How long we keep it, and how you delete it

We keep your data while your account is active. **You can delete everything yourself: Settings →
Delete account.** That permanently removes your family's profile, conversations, companions, and
plan — including from our backups, which are refreshed immediately. One anonymous entry (that a
deletion happened, and when) is kept for our records; it contains none of your family's content.
You can also export your family's plan as a spreadsheet at any time from the web dashboard.
