# Classes & sports

Camps fill weeks; classes and sports fill *seasons* — soccer on Tuesdays,
swim on Thursdays, piano after school. MVP treats them as what they are:
weekly commitments with a seasonal registration moment.

## Weekly, not week-by-week

Tell the assistant the schedule the way you'd say it:

> "Mar's doing soccer this fall — every Tuesday at 4, $200 for the season."

That lands on the board as one card with its rhythm ("Tuesdays 4:00 PM
through Dec 15"), and the weekly slot feeds conflict detection — if a camp
week or an appointment collides with practice, you hear about it before it
happens. Change the schedule ("practice moved to Thursdays") and the
calendar reconciles itself.

## Registration is seasonal

Fall and spring sign-up windows open, fill, and close fast. When you mention
a class you're considering, the assistant treats the registration timing as
the thing to nail down — an interest with a missed window is a sad kid.

## Same lifecycle as everything else

Considering → decided → registered → all set. Once registered, the fee
to-do is seeded, and — with Gmail connected — confirmation and start-date
emails are watched so the first-day details don't get lost in your inbox.
