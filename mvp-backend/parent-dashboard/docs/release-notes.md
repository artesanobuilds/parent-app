# What's new

Every update we ship, newest first — what changed and how to find it. Features marked
*app update* arrive on your phone through TestFlight; everything else is live the moment
it's listed here.

## August 8, 2026 — the design, properly

**MVP now looks the way it was designed** *(app update 1.80)* — the last two updates were built
from screenshots of the design rather than the design itself, and a lot of small things were
slightly off. The bottom tab bar is rebuilt: the selected tab is now a soft rounded highlight
around the whole item, and **Plan is now Planner**. Section headings on Home (**YOUR KIDS**,
**FOR YOU**, **COMING UP**) were in the wrong typeface entirely and now match. And the sheet
behind your photo — saved articles, history, your family's interests — has the right colours,
corners and type throughout.

**Home leads with your kids** *(app update 1.80)* — the order was wrong: your kids' cards were
below the coach's note, and your reading picks were at the very bottom. It now goes your kids,
then your coach, then reading, then what's coming up.

**Fixed: the last line of Coach's disclaimer was cut off** *(app update 1.80)* — on the Coach
screen, "Coach can make mistakes and is not a crisis line" was clipped by the tab bar so the
"Learn more" link couldn't be tapped. Both are fully visible now.

## August 7, 2026 — one coach, and a clearer Plan tab

**"Chief of Staff" is just your coach now** *(app update 1.79)* — there were two assistants
with two separate memories, and you had to pick the right tab before you could ask. There's
one. The Plan tab now says **Plan | Ask the coach**, and it's the same coach that knows your
family, your Circle, and your plan. Ask about camps or about a bedtime meltdown in either
place; the answer comes from the same place.

**The Plan tab was reorganised, not rebuilt** *(app update 1.79)* — everything that was there
is still there, in a more useful order. One line at the top tells you where things stand ("2
next steps this week · $0 confirmed · 1 cost TBD · 2 tracked") and **View** expands it.
**List | Timeline** switches between your board and your week-by-week calendar instead of
being separate tabs. Filter chips — **All / New / Deciding / Needs action** — narrow the board,
and the search form is tucked into one row ("98332 · 25 mi · camps, classes & more") until you
need it.

**Every activity now tells you why it's there** *(app update 1.79)* — open any activity and
you'll see a **% match** and a plain-language **"Why it matched"**: *"Weekly drop-in fits your
flexible-weeks preference, Lily's age band, and it's the closest full-day option to home."*
The score was always being computed; it just got thrown away before you could see it.

**Overlaps get flagged before you register** *(live now)* — if a camp collides with a trip
you've already planned for the same kid, the dates row says so: *"⚠ Overlaps Rialto Beach trip,
Aug 15–17."* Previously we only checked calendar events against recurring appointments, so two
planned activities could quietly land on the same week.

**"Still to do" shows the whole list** *(app update 1.79)* — an activity used to show only its
single next step. Now you see every task with the finished ones struck through, so you can tell
what's left at a glance. "Mark next step done" still moves it along, and it moves to **All set**
once nothing is left.

## August 7, 2026 — Circle is your people now

**The community board is gone, and Circle is private** *(app update 1.78)* — Circle used to
be a public board where you saw posts from parents you'd never met. It's now the people *you*
invite: a co-parent, a grandparent, a nanny, the friend who does Thursday pickup. Open
**Circle → Invite someone** and send the link however you already talk to that person. Full
details in [Your Circle](/dashboard/docs/circle).

**Conversations with the people who actually help** *(app update 1.78)* — ask a question,
pick who to include, and get replies in one thread. You might ask the grandparents about the
weekend without looping in the nanny. Only the person who started a conversation can rename or
delete it.

**Nobody can reach you unless you've added them too** *(live now)* — a conversation is only
possible when you *both* have each other in your Circle. Adding someone one-sidedly does
nothing on their end. **If you were using direct messages with another parent, those threads
are no longer reachable** — parent-to-parent DMs came out with the board. Invite that person
to your Circle and you can pick the conversation back up there.

**Your account, behind your photo** *(app update 1.78)* — tap your picture on Home. Your
saved articles, your history, the things your family likes doing, and your account details are
all there. **Saved moved out of Settings** and into this sheet, which is where people kept
looking for it.

## August 7, 2026 — a new look, and a head start

**MVP looks different** *(app update 1.76)* — new colours, new type, and a calmer layout
throughout. The biggest change is the first screen you land on: instead of a photo banner and
a grid of topics, Home is now a dashboard. It opens with what your coach has to say this
morning, then each of your kids, then what's coming up, then reading picked for you. Nothing
to turn on — update the app and it's there.

**Signing up is shorter** *(app update 1.76)* — the old signup asked about your postal code,
age range, work pattern, pronouns, cultural background, your energy levels and your support
network before you'd seen anything. That's gone. It now asks your name, your household, your
language, then your kids one at a time, then what's hardest right now. Your coach picks up the
rest naturally as you talk to it, instead of demanding it up front from a stranger.

**Your coach and your plan get ready while you finish signing up** *(app update 1.76)* — there's
a short "creating account" step at the end with a progress ring. It isn't decoration. Behind it
your coach is being brought up to speed on your family and a first plan is being sketched, so
the app has something real for you the moment you arrive instead of an empty screen. If any of
it can't finish, you go straight through anyway — you'll never be stuck waiting on it.

**Five tabs instead of six** *(app update 1.76)* — Home, Coach, Plan, Circle, Settings. The
Library and your Saved items moved into **Settings**, under "Reading". The quickest way to
articles is now **Home → For you → See all**, which is the whole library reordered around your
family rather than sorted by topic.

**Your kid's page got a rebuild** *(app update 1.76)* — tap the **•••** on any kid on Home, or
find them in **Settings → Profile**. Their photo, age, personality, what your coach has noticed
about them lately, their companion, and what we've learned about their interests, strengths and
challenges — all on one screen, all editable in place.

**Your coach and your planner are now one assistant** *(live now)* — they were two, with two
separate memories, and you had to pick the right tab before you could ask. Now it's one: ask
about camps in **Coach**, ask about a bedtime meltdown in the planner chat, and either way
you're talking to the same assistant with the same memory of your family. The turns that were
worst before are the ones that get better — "Ben's summer is a mess" is a coverage problem *and*
an overwhelmed parent, and you now get one answer to the whole thing. It also knows your
**Circle** now, so "can Rosa cover Thursday?" is answerable. Nothing to turn on.

**Articles come to you instead of you going to them** *(live now)* — your coach knows the whole
library and offers an article when one actually fits what you're describing, ranked for your
family. If you'd rather browse, the same ranked list is at **Home → For you → See all**.

**Signup asks a few things it should have all along** *(app update 1.77)* — where you live (so
we can find camps and classes that are actually near you), how your kids learn, whether you speak
more than one language at home, and a quick pass on what your family does with its free time.
There's also a new step to set how you want your coach to sound. All of it is skippable.

**Home looks different before you've set anyone up** *(app update 1.77)* — a brand-new family
used to land on a dashboard with empty sections and no explanation. Now it tells you what to do
next: a prompt to introduce your kids, and a way to add your first event.

## July 24, 2026 — a coach that remembers you're there

**Coach greets *you*, not "a user"** *(live now)* — voice conversations used to open with the
same "Hello! How can I help you today?" every single time. Now Coach greets you by name and,
if you've talked before, picks up the thread — "want to continue where we left off, or is
something new going on?" Just open voice; the first words are already different.

**Going quiet no longer confuses Coach** *(live now)* — if you paused to think, Coach used to
fill the silence by repeating its last answer. Now a pause gets a gentle "still there? no
rush" — and if you stay quiet, Coach simply waits with you instead of talking at you. Your
kid's companion does the same, kid-sized ("are you still there?").

## July 23, 2026 — voice that reads along with you

**Words now appear as they're spoken** *(app update 1.74)* — when you talk to Coach by voice,
the reply used to drop onto the screen as one finished paragraph while the voice was still on
its first sentence. Now the text arrives in step with the voice, a few words at a time, so you
can read along instead of reading ahead. Same on your kid's companion. Nothing to turn on —
just open voice and talk.

**Coach starts talking sooner** *(live now)* — we sped up the voice itself, and when Coach
needs a moment to look something up (an article, your plan, the calendar) it now says so out
loud instead of going silent. Fewer "is this thing on?" pauses.

**Interrupting leaves the right words on screen** *(app update 1.74)* — if you cut Coach off
mid-sentence, the screen now trims back to what was actually said out loud, instead of leaving
the rest of a reply you never heard.

## July 22, 2026 — voice, healed

**Voice conversations now recover on their own** *(live now)* — a few of you hit a
"Sorry — something went wrong" wall when talking to Coach by voice. We found the cause: if a
voice reply got interrupted at exactly the wrong moment (say, you started talking while Coach
was mid-lookup), the conversation could get stuck and *stay* stuck. Coach now repairs the
conversation automatically before every reply, and if something still goes sideways it quietly
retries before saying anything. If voice ever told you "something went wrong" — it's fixed,
just open voice and talk.

## July 19, 2026 — messages, sharpened

**Reactions, instant delivery, and a smarter envelope** *(app update 1.73)* — four upgrades to
parent-to-parent messages, straight from beta feedback:

- **React to messages** — press and hold any message to add a reaction (❤️ 👍 👎 😂 ‼️ ❓),
  just like iMessage. Tap yours again to remove it.
- **Messages appear instantly** — when someone writes back while you're in the conversation, the
  message shows up the moment your phone buzzes (and the notification banner stays out of your way,
  since you're already reading the thread).
- **Notifications open the conversation** — tapping a message notification now takes you straight
  into that thread instead of dropping you on the home screen.
- **You can see you have messages from anywhere** — the **Circle** tab in the bottom bar shows an
  unread count, and inside Circle the envelope lights up with the number waiting.

## July 19, 2026 — the Saved tab

**Save anything for later** *(app update 1.72)* — a new **Saved** tab (the bookmark icon in
the bottom bar) collects everything you bookmark across the app: tap the bookmark on any
**library article** or **community post**, or the bookmark in the **Coach** chat to keep
Coach's last reply. Saved coach replies expand right in the list — no digging back through
the conversation. Your Plan-tab shortlist shows there too, so one place holds everything
you're coming back to. Swipe to unsave. What you save also quietly helps Coach understand
what you actually care about — that's covered in the updated
[Privacy Policy](/dashboard/docs/privacy-policy).

## July 19, 2026

**Message other parents** *(app update 1.71)* — MVP now has simple direct messages, so you
can actually talk to the parents you meet in the community. Two ways in: tap the **envelope
icon** at the top of the Circle tab to see your inbox and browse the parent directory, or tap
the small envelope on any **community post or reply** to message its author directly. Unread
counts show on the envelope, you'll get a notification when someone writes back, and every
conversation has **Report** and **Block** one tap away (the ⋯ menu). Messages are covered in
the [Privacy Policy](/dashboard/docs/privacy-policy) — stored so both of you can read the
thread, gone when either of you deletes your account.

**Legal pages read better on your phone** *(live now)* — opening the Privacy Policy or Terms
from the app now lands you straight on the document instead of a long list of links. The docs
navigation moved below the text on phones:

## July 18, 2026

**Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, published** *(live now)* — real, plain-language legal
documents: [Privacy Policy](/dashboard/docs/privacy-policy) and
[Terms of Service](/dashboard/docs/terms). Linked from **Settings → Legal** in the app, and
versioned — the version and effective date sit at the bottom of each page, and if we ever
change something that matters, the app will show you what changed and ask you to agree.

**Profile photos** *(app update 1.70)* — add a photo for yourself and each of your kids:
**Settings → your name → Photo**, or open any kid's profile. Photos appear on your Home
greeting and in Settings. Photo library only, stored privately, and deleted with the rest of
your data if you ever delete your account.

**Your coach is now "Coach"** *(app update 1.69)* — the coach tab finally introduces itself
properly, and when Coach recommends an article or a community conversation, **the link now
opens it** — tap any citation in a coach reply to jump straight to the piece.

![Coach — same warmth, now with working links to the library.](/dashboard/docs-assets/ios-coach.png)

**Log out, delete account, and a cleaner Settings** *(app updates 1.67–1.68)* — Settings was
rebuilt around what you actually need: your profile, connections, **Report a problem**, the
legal pages, and — at last — **Log out** and **Delete account**. Deleting is immediate and
self-serve: your family's data is removed right away, backups included. And when you come
back after logging out, the sign-in screen greets you by name.
