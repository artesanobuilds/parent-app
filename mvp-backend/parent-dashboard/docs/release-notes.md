# What's new

Every update, newest first — what changed and how to find it. Each entry says
whether it is available now or prepared for an upcoming rollout. Features marked
*app update* arrive on your phone through TestFlight.

## October 11, 2026 — more variety while your companion thinks

**Live now; no app update required.** Start a fresh conversation from **Home →
Hand to kid**. Thinking phrases now draw from 8 short options instead of three,
with the same one-second timing. The random selection may still repeat a phrase.

## October 11, 2026 — earlier companion feedback

**Live now; no app update required.** Start a fresh conversation from **Home →
Hand to kid**. When a reply takes time, the companion can begin a short thinking
phrase after one second instead of 2.5 seconds. This reduces silent waiting;
the answer still passes through the same safety checks.

## October 11, 2026 — smoother companion conversations

**Live with this backend rollout; no app update required.** From **Home → Hand
to kid**, open an enabled companion as usual. Spoken replies now consistently
use short, natural sentences, and companions use your child's current profile
while keeping the boundaries you approved. Returning children get a returning
welcome. Safety checks run without blocking other conversations, and temporary
service problems get a clearer retry message.

## October 10, 2026 — searches and setup recover more reliably

**Available with the next backend rollout; no app update required.** Canceling
an activity search prevents that attempt from publishing late results. Searches
that run out of time finish with an error you can retry. Start searches from
your existing **Coach** or activity-planning screen. During account setup, an
unavailable preparation step no longer keeps every later step waiting; continue
through the existing welcome flow as usual.

## October 10, 2026 — continue the selected coaching conversation

**Available after the backend rollout is enabled; no app update required.**
Text planning messages will continue the conversation selected in **Coach**,
including a conversation reopened from the clock icon. Start a new conversation
with the pencil icon when changing topics. Existing conversation history stays
available; voice calls keep their separate history.

## October 10, 2026 — more reliable coaching and planning saves

**Available with the next backend rollout; no app update required.** The coach
saves family details before confirming them in text or voice. If an interrupted
conversation resumes a known planning action, the saved action is recovered
instead of added twice. Open **Coach** and type or speak as usual; saved family
details remain available in **Settings → Profile**, and planning items remain
on your existing activity and to-do screens.

## August 21, 2026 — a brand-new welcome, and a Home built around today

**Getting started is completely redesigned** *(app update 1.90)* — the coach now shows you
what it can do before you create an account. Tell it who's on your calendar, pick one thing
to hand over, then paste a real message — a team email, a group text, a photographed flyer —
or just dictate it, and watch the coach turn it into a structured plan card: who, what,
when, where. You check its work, it introduces itself with your family's actual details,
and only then does it ask you to sign in. Your account is created at the end, not the start.

**Speak instead of type** *(app update 1.90)* — every text box in the new welcome flow has a
microphone. Dictation happens on your device; audio never leaves your phone.

**The coach asks about you, too** *(app update 1.90)* — one new step asks "What about you —
what's your to-do this week?" Gifts to buy, errands, a workout, date night. Your own
to-dos get a card on Home right next to the kids' plans, because taking care of the parent
is the point.

**Home now opens with today** *(app update 1.90)* — a rebuilt Home screen greets you with
today's date and one card per person: each kid's upcoming activities, your own to-dos, a
note from your coach, your kids, and fresh MVP Editorial reads — all in one scroll.

**Already have an account? The new welcome now merges** *(app update 1.91)* — if you walk
the new welcome flow and sign in to an existing account at the end, everything you just
entered joins your family instead of disappearing: kids with the same name merge, new
names are added, and nothing gets duplicated.

**Finding activities is faster** — searching for camps and classes now typically takes under
two minutes instead of three, with the same quality of matches.

**Saving an activity no longer hides your search results** *(app update 1.88)* — tapping Save
used to clear the whole results list off the screen and leave the search box broken. Now your
results stay visible, the saved card shows a checkmark, and you can keep browsing or search
again right where you are.

**Activity results and saved plan items now show full details** *(app update 1.88)* — match
percentage, price, schedule, and location are all visible on the card and in the detail view,
and a saved activity keeps that information instead of showing just the basics.

**One splash screen, not two** *(app update 1.88)* — opening the app used to flash the old "MVP"
wordmark before showing a different illustration a moment later. Now it's the same artwork the
whole way through, from the moment the app opens.

**Circle's empty state is personalized** *(app update 1.88)* — before you've added anyone,
Circle now greets you with your kids' names instead of generic text.

**Polish across Home, Planner, and the tab bar** *(app update 1.88)* — the bottom navigation bar,
Home's family and coach cards, and Planner's search and detail screens have all been tightened
up to match the app's design more closely.

**Deleting your account now removes everything, immediately and permanently** — we found and
closed a gap where coach conversations could briefly outlive an account deletion in our backups.
They no longer do.

**Re-doing setup is smoother** *(app update 1.88)* — if you delete your account and start over,
the questionnaire no longer loops you back to the plan screen after the interests step, and the
splash artwork shows once at launch instead of popping up again mid-setup.

## August 8, 2026 — fixed: your kids disappearing from Home

**Fixed: leaving Home and coming back could show no kids** *(app update 1.83)* — switching to
another tab and returning sometimes left YOUR KIDS showing the "add your first child" message,
as though your children weren't there. **Nothing was ever lost** — it was a loading fault, and
the data was on the server the whole time.

Two things caused it, and both are fixed. Returning to Home restarts the roster fetch, and the
restart was cancelling the first one before it finished. And when the fetch didn't complete, the
screen showed the same "no kids yet" message a brand-new family sees. Now overlapping refreshes
wait for each other instead of cancelling, and if the roster genuinely can't be loaded you get
**"Couldn't load your kids — they're safe, this is a connection problem, not missing data"** with
a **Try again**, rather than a screen that implies your family is gone.

## August 8, 2026 — adding someone by email, and a real Contacts screen

**Add someone to your Circle by email — no link to send** *(app update 1.82)* — a nanny or a
grandparent may already be on MVP, because they're already in someone else's circle. Open
**Circle → Contacts → add contact**, type their email, and we handle both cases: if they have
an account they get a request in their own Circle, and if they don't we set up an invite link
for you to share. Either way you see the same confirmation.

Two deliberate details. **We never tell you whether an email is already on MVP** — if we did,
anyone could type addresses into the app to find out who has an account. And **the other person
always has to accept**: no one lands in your Circle, or can start a conversation with you,
until they say yes. You'll see requests waiting for *you* at the top of the Circle tab, with
**Accept** and **Decline**.

**A proper Contacts screen** *(app update 1.82)* — your people in one list, with the ones
you're still waiting on marked as such, and a **⋯** on each row to copy their invite link, edit
their profile, or remove them. Select several and **Invite selected** does them in one pass.

**Everyone in your Circle now has a profile you can fill in** *(app update 1.82)* — tap a
contact to record how they fit into your family: their relation, what they're good with, what
they find hard, what you'd like them to focus on. A co-parent additionally gets **Co-Parent
goals**; a nanny, grandparent or friend gets a **Personality** — Spirited, Easy-going or
Sensitive. Your coach reads these, so a question about handoffs or bedtime can account for who
is actually doing them.

## August 8, 2026 — finishing your family's details, one person at a time

**Your account, behind your photo** *(app update 1.78)* — tap your picture on Home. Your

## July 24, 2026 — a coach that remembers you're there

**Coach greets *you*, not "a user"** *(live now)* — voice conversations used to open with the

## July 23, 2026 — voice that reads along with you

**Words now appear as they're spoken** *(app update 1.74)* — when you talk to Coach by voice,
the reply used to drop onto the screen as one finished paragraph while the voice was still on

**Coach starts talking sooner** *(live now)* — we sped up the voice itself, and when Coach

**Interrupting leaves the right words on screen** *(app update 1.74)* — if you cut Coach off

## July 22, 2026 — voice, healed

**Voice conversations now recover on their own** *(live now)* — a few of you hit a
"Sorry — something went wrong" wall when talking to Coach by voice. We found the cause: if a
voice reply got interrupted at exactly the wrong moment (say, you started talking while Coach
was mid-lookup), the conversation could get stuck and *stay* stuck. Coach now repairs the
conversation automatically before every reply, and if something still goes sideways it quietly
retries before saying anything. If voice ever told you "something went wrong" — it's fixed,
just open voice and talk.

**Reactions, instant delivery, and a smarter envelope** *(app update 1.73)* — four upgrades to

- **You can see you have messages from anywhere** — the **Circle** tab in the bottom bar shows an

## July 19, 2026
