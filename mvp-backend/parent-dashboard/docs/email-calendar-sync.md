# Email & calendar sync

Both connections are optional, off by default, and scoped to the job.

## Gmail — the watcher

Once connected (Settings → Integrations in the iPhone app), MVP watches for
emails from the providers of activities you've **registered** — confirmations,
payment reminders, forms, schedule changes — and turns them into to-dos and
card updates. Watching starts the moment you mark an activity registered.

What it doesn't do:

- It doesn't read your whole inbox looking for things to do — it looks for
  mail related to the activities you're tracking.
- It never sends anything from your account.

No Gmail? Paste or forward a confirmation email into the app and it pulls out
the same dates, payments, and forms by hand.

## Google Calendar — two-way

Connect Google Calendar and plan entries (camp weeks, classes, blackout
weeks) push to a calendar you choose, and outside events pull in so schedule
conflicts are caught against your real life, not just the app's view of it.

Removing an entry in MVP removes the copy it pushed; events that came *from*
your Google Calendar are never deleted by MVP.
