# Privacy & your data

MVP is in closed beta with real families, so the data practices are simple
and conservative:

- **Your plan is yours.** Kids' names, ages, interests, and your activity
  plans exist to run your plan — they're never sold and never used for ads.
- **Connections are scoped.** Gmail watching looks for mail about activities
  you registered; calendar sync touches the calendar you picked. Both are
  optional and can be disconnected anytime in the iPhone app.
- **Drafts, not sends.** The assistant never sends email from your account —
  it writes drafts you review and send yourself.
- **Export and deletion.** You can export your plan as a spreadsheet anytime.
  If you want your family's data deleted, ask and it's removed — the account
  and everything attached to it.

Questions or concerns during the beta? Use **Report a problem** in the iPhone
app's settings — it goes straight to the person building this.
