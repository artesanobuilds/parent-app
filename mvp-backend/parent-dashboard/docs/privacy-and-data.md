# Privacy & your data

MVP is in closed beta with real families, so the data practices are simple
and conservative. This page is the plain-language summary — the full documents
are the [Privacy Policy](/dashboard/docs/privacy-policy) and the
[Terms of Service](/dashboard/docs/terms), both versioned so you can always
see exactly what you agreed to.

- **Your plan is yours.** Kids' names, ages, interests, and your activity
  plans exist to run your plan — they're never sold, never used for ads, and
  never used to train AI models.
- **Connections are scoped.** Gmail watching looks for mail about activities
  you registered; calendar sync touches the calendar you picked. Both are
  optional and can be disconnected anytime in the iPhone app.
- **Drafts, not sends.** The assistant never sends email from your account —
  it writes drafts you review and send yourself.
- **Kid companions are parent-controlled.** They exist only after your
  explicit consent, and you can read every message your child exchanges.
  Safety concerns lock the conversation and notify you immediately.
- **Export and deletion are self-serve.** You can export your plan as a
  spreadsheet anytime. To delete everything, it's **Settings → Delete
  account** in the iPhone app — your family's data is removed immediately,
  including from backups. No email, no waiting.

Questions or concerns during the beta? Use **Report a problem** in the iPhone
app's settings — it goes straight to the person building this.
