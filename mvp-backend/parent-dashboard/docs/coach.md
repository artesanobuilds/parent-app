# The parenting coach

The plan board handles the logistics of parenting. The coach handles the rest
of it — the bedtime meltdowns, the sibling wars, the "am I doing this right?"
at 11pm. It lives in the iPhone app, in the **Coach** tab.

## What a conversation looks like

Bring it a real moment, the way you'd tell a friend:

![The coach answers with something you can do tonight — not a lecture.](/dashboard/docs-assets/ios-coach.png)

The coach's style is deliberate: name what's actually happening, then give
you two or three concrete things to try *tonight*, sized to your kid's age.
No parenting-book chapters, no judgment.

## It knows your family — because you told it

The coach reads the same family profile as everything else in MVP: your
kids' ages, temperaments, what's been hard lately. When you mention something
new — "the meltdowns started this week" — it quietly updates the profile, so
next week's conversation starts from where you actually are, not from zero.

You can see and edit everything it knows in **Profile**. Nothing is inferred
from anywhere else; the profile is what you've said, and it's yours to
correct or delete.

## Voice

The waveform button starts a voice conversation with the same coach — useful
when your hands are full, which is most of the time.

## What it isn't

The coach is an AI, and the app says so right under the input: it can make
mistakes, and **it is not a crisis line**. For emergencies or concerns about
anyone's safety, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.
