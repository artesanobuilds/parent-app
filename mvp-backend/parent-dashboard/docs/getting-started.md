# Getting started

MVP is a chief of staff for your kids' activities. It keeps the whole
job in one place — finding options, deciding, registering, and staying on top
of everything that follows (payments, forms, schedules, who's driving) — so
you don't have to hold it all in your head.

There are two ways to use it:

- **The iPhone app** — the full experience: the plan board, the planning
  assistant, discovery, uploads, and your family profile.
- **This web dashboard** — the same plan on a big screen: the board, discovery
  results, and the assistant in a browser.

## Signing in

- **Sign in with Google** — one tap on the login page.
- **Pairing code** — in the iPhone app, open Settings and mint a pairing code,
  then type it on the login page. Your phone and the dashboard share one plan.

## The one-minute tour

1. **Plan** is your board. Every activity you're tracking sits in one of four
   columns — *Considering*, *To register*, *Registered · needs action*, and
   *All set* — and each card shows exactly one next step.
2. **Discovery** is where found options land when you ask the assistant to go
   look ("find art camps near us").
3. **Chat** is the assistant itself. Anything you can do with buttons you can
   also just say.

![The plan board — four stages, and every card carries its own next step.](/dashboard/docs-assets/plan-board.png)

A good first ask: *"What can you actually do for me?"*

## And on the phone
