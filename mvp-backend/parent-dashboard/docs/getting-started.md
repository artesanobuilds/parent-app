# Getting started

MVP is one coach for the whole job of raising your kids — the hard parenting
questions and the logistics behind them. It keeps it all in one place: finding
options, deciding, registering, and staying on top of everything that follows
(payments, forms, schedules, who's driving) — so you don't have to hold it in
your head.

There are two ways to use it:

- **The iPhone app** — the full experience: the plan board, the planning
  assistant, discovery, uploads, and your family profile.
- **This web dashboard** — the same plan on a big screen: the board, discovery
  results, and the assistant in a browser.

## Signing in

- **Sign in with Google** — one tap on the login page.
- **Pairing code** — in the iPhone app, open Settings and mint a pairing code,
  then type it on the login page. Your phone and the dashboard share one plan.

## The one-minute tour

1. **Plan** is your board. Every activity you're tracking sits in one of four
   columns — *Considering*, *To register*, *Registered · needs action*, and
   *All set* — and each card shows exactly one next step.
2. **Discovery** is where found options land when you ask the assistant to go
   look ("find art camps near us").
3. **Chat** is the assistant itself. Anything you can do with buttons you can
   also just say.

![The plan board — four stages, and every card carries its own next step.](/dashboard/docs-assets/plan-board.png)

A good first ask: *"What can you actually do for me?"*

## And on the phone

The iPhone app carries the same plan, plus the parts that live best in your
pocket: the [parenting coach](coach) and your kids'
[companions](companions).

## Filling in your family

The app introduces itself before it asks you to sign up *(app update 1.90)*: tell the
coach who's on your calendar, hand it one real thing — a team email, a group text, a
photographed flyer, or just say it out loud — and watch it come back as a plan card
(who, what, when, where). You check its work, the coach introduces itself using your
family's actual details, and only then do you create the account. Everything you entered
comes with you.

The details that make the coach's advice specific — each child's grade, school style,
what they're into and where they shine, plus your household and language — come right
after, as one "Tell me about…" pass the first time you land on Home.

Nothing about it is compulsory. **Set up later** skips it, Home works either way, and
you can come back to any person later:

- Tap **Set up {name}** on a child's tile on Home.
- Tap the **⋯** on a child's tile and choose **Finish setting up {name}**.
- Or go to **Settings**, and tap the child under "Your family" — same editors.

Close the app mid-way and it resumes where you were.

The final setup progress screen keeps moving if a personalization step takes too
long. You can still reach **Home**, and a timed-out step cannot overwrite your
setup later. Your saved family details remain available in **Settings → Your
family**.

**Adding a child** is on Home too — **+ Add another child**, under the tiles (or
**Settings → Your family → + Add a kid**; they're the same thing).
