# The planning assistant

The planning assistant **is your coach** — same assistant, same memory. It used
to be a separate one that only knew logistics; now anything on this page can be
asked in either place, and what you tell one, the other knows. See
[the parenting coach](coach).

It can do everything the buttons can do, plus the parts that need judgment.
Talk to it the way you'd brief a very organized friend.

![A fresh chat opens with the four doors — camps, classes, outings, trips — each a one-tap way in.](/dashboard/docs-assets/chat-fresh.png)

## Things it's good at

**Keeping the picture.**
- "Where are my coverage gaps this summer?"
- "What's still pending or needs confirming?"
- "What's our total camp spend so far?"

**Finding options.**
- "Find soccer camps near us for July." It searches the web in the
  background and the finds land in Discovery — nothing is added to your board
  without you saying so.

**Deciding.**
- "Help me decide between the camps on my shortlist." It lays out cost,
  age-eligibility, schedule clashes, and which options compete for the same
  week — and gives you a recommendation, not just a table.

**Saving what you already did.**
- "I signed up for Camp Galileo last night." It looks the camp up, saves it
  as registered, and starts tracking what follows.

![One sentence in, and the class is on the board with a registration to-do — the assistant tells you what it did and what's still open.](/dashboard/docs-assets/chat-conversation.png)

**The follow-through.**
- "Add a to-do to call the dentist." · "Grandma can do pickups on Fridays." ·
  "Block out the week of July 20 — we'll be at grandma's."

**Quick weekend ideas.**
- "What can we do this weekend?" — see [Weekend outings](outing).

## What it won't do

- **It never sends email.** Ask it to draft a note to a camp director and it
  hands you the draft — you review and send it yourself.
- **It doesn't register or pay for you.** It gets you to the right page with
  the right information and tracks what's left.
