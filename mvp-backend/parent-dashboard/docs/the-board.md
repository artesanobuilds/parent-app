# The plan board

The board is the single source of truth for where every activity stands. Four
stages, in the order things actually happen:

| Stage | What it means | The card's next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Considering** | Saved ideas — nothing decided. | "We'll do it" moves it forward. |
| **To register** | You said yes, but haven't signed up. | "Register" opens the signup page; then "Mark registered". |
| **Registered · needs action** | Signed up, but something's still open. | The top open to-do (pay, forms). |
| **All set** | Registered and nothing left to do. | Collapsed — done is done. |

Two extra buckets appear only when needed: **Waiting / blocked** (something
needs confirming first — eligibility, a waitlist) and **Not doing** (things
you passed on — restorable anytime).

## What happens when you register

Marking an activity **registered** is the moment MVP starts working for you:

- If Gmail is connected, it starts **watching that provider's emails** for
  confirmations, payment reminders, and forms — the card shows a "watching"
  line so you know it's on.
- It seeds the obvious to-dos: a **payment** to-do when the price is known,
  and a "forward the confirmation email" to-do when Gmail isn't connected.

## Passing and starting over

- **Pass** moves a card to Not doing and dismisses its open to-dos — an
  abandoned plan shouldn't leave zombie "Pay $450" reminders around.
- **Delete forever** removes the card and everything attached to it.
- **Start over** (in the board menu) wipes the whole plan — activities,
  to-dos, calendar — but keeps your family profile.

## Dates on cards

Each card shows its schedule the way you'd say it — "Week of Jul 13" for a
camp week, "Tuesdays 4:00 PM" for a weekly class. Weeks with no camp and no
adult covering a kid show up as **coverage gaps** in the plan summary.
