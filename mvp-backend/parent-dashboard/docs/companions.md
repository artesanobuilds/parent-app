# Kids' companions

A companion is a small, warm AI friend you create *for* your kid — named by
you, shaped around what they love, and always under your control. Companions
live on the Home tab of the iPhone app.

![Each kid gets their own companion card on Home — set up, active, and always visible to you.](/dashboard/docs-assets/ios-home.png)

## You build it, you consent to it

A companion doesn't exist until you set it up: you write a short brief about
your kid ("loves art, octopuses, and her soccer team"), and you give explicit
parental consent before any kid can chat. No consent, no companion — the
screen simply won't open.

## Handing the phone over

When it's your kid's turn, tap **Hand to kid**. The app switches into kid
mode — a simplified, locked-down surface with just their friend in it:

![Kid mode starts with "Who's using the device?" — each kid gets only their own companion.](/dashboard/docs-assets/ios-kid-picker.png)

## What a companion conversation looks like

Companions are relentlessly wholesome: facts, drawing challenges, silly
games, encouragement — tuned to the brief you wrote and your kid's age.

![Inky, built from a one-line brief: octopus facts on demand, always age-sized.](/dashboard/docs-assets/ios-companion.png)

## The safety layer (always on)

Incoming messages are checked before the companion answers. Responses are
checked while they stream and again when complete; a late check may stop a
response after some words have already appeared or been spoken:

- **Gentle redirects.** Off-track topics get a soft, age-appropriate redirect
  rather than engagement.
- **Crisis lock.** If a conversation ever touches something serious — safety,
  self-harm, abuse — the companion **freezes immediately**, the kid sees a
  caring hand-off message, and **you get a push notification** right away.
  The companion stays locked until you've reviewed what happened and choose
  to reactivate it.
- **You can always read the conversation.** Transcripts are available to
  you, the parent — companions are never a private channel between your kid
  and an AI.

## Voice

Companions can also talk. The same safety layer applies to voice
conversations, word for word.

Spoken replies use short, natural sentences. The companion uses your child's
current profile and keeps the topics and boundaries you approved. Returning
children receive a welcome back. If a safety-check service is unavailable, the
companion asks you to try again rather than treating the question as wrongdoing.

**Hand to kid** opens the child's own companion. It does not share your private
coaching conversation or automatically tell the companion what you were discussing.
