# Your Circle

Circle is the people who help you raise your kids — a co-parent, a grandparent,
a nanny, the friend who does the Thursday pickup. You build it yourself, one
person at a time, and nobody is in it unless you put them there.

It used to be a public message board where you could see posts from parents you'd
never met. That's gone. Circle is now private.

## Adding someone

Open **Circle** and tap **Invite someone**. You get a link you can send however
you already talk to that person — text, WhatsApp, email. When they open it they
see who invited them and what it's for, and they can join.

You can also add someone as a plain contact without inviting them — useful for a
sitter you want on the list before you're ready to give them app access. They
just won't see anything until they accept an invite.

Nothing you add is shared anywhere. A person in your Circle can't see your other
Circle members' details, your plan, or your coach conversations.

## Conversations

A conversation is a question you put to some or all of your Circle, and their
replies. Tap **+**, give it a title and a first message, then choose who to
include — you might ask the grandparents about weekend plans without looping in
the nanny.

Everyone you included can reply. Everyone can see the replies. You can add more
people later; they'll see the conversation from the beginning.

Only the person who started a conversation can rename or delete it.

## Who can message you

Someone can only be in a conversation with you if **you both have each other in
your Circle**. Adding a person one-sidedly does nothing on their end — they
won't see you, and they can't reach you. That's deliberate: it means nobody can
type your name into their own list and start reading your replies.

If you had direct messages with another parent before this update, those threads
are no longer reachable — the old parent-to-parent messaging came out with the
board. Nothing was deleted on our side, but the way to talk to someone now is to
invite them to your Circle.

## Leaving and removing

Remove someone from your Circle and they lose access to every conversation you
share. Delete your account and every conversation you started goes with it,
along with your messages in anyone else's — see the
[Privacy Policy](/dashboard/docs/privacy-policy).
