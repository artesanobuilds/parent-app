# The registration watchlist

The worst parenting-logistics failure isn't picking the wrong camp — it's
finding the *right* one and missing the day signups opened. The watchlist
exists so that never happens.

## How it works

Tell the assistant when a registration opens, in whatever words:

> "Rec soccer signup opens August 12 — don't let me miss it."

From that moment the window is **watched**. You get two nudges:

1. **A week out** — "opens Aug 12, get your details ready."
2. **The day it opens** — before it opens, never after. If you told us a
   date without a time, the heads-up comes the evening before.

Every reminder arrives as a push notification *and* a to-do on your board —
so even with notifications off, the board catches it. Cards with a watched
window show it plainly: *"Registration opens Aug 12 · watching."*

## Dates we found vs. dates you told us

- A date **you** gave us (or one from a confirmation email) is a promise we
  keep, period.
- A date the assistant **found on the web** is labeled *unconfirmed* — and
  the week-out nudge asks you to verify it with one tap. We'd rather ask
  than let a scraped date quietly be wrong.

## Changing or dismissing

Registration moved? Say so ("actually it opens the 19th") — the reminders
re-arm around the new date automatically. Already registered or don't care
anymore? Dismiss the window and it goes quiet.
