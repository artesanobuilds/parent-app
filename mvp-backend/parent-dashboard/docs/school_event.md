# School stuff

The PTA newsletter is the densest logistics document in family life: one
email can hold picture day, a fundraiser, a permission slip, *and* spirit
week — each with its own deadline. MVP unpacks it.

## Snap it, paste it, done

Forward the newsletter, paste the email, or photograph the flyer (Add info in
the iPhone app). Every event comes out as its own item with its own to-dos:

- **Permission slip** → a *sign and return* to-do with the due date
- **Fee notice** → a *pay* to-do with the amount and deadline
- **Picture day / spirit day** → the date on your calendar and a prep to-do
- **Fundraiser** → a *decide: in or out* to-do

## Every deadline shows its receipts

Extraction from photos and newsletters isn't perfect — so every extracted
deadline is shown **next to the exact sentence it came from**:

> Pay $25 by Mar 14 — *from: "field trip fees are due by Friday March 14th"*

If the quote doesn't match the to-do, you'll see it instantly and can fix it
in one tap. Nothing extracted is ever auto-confirmed: items land as
*Considering* / pending for your review, always.

## Single days, not week blocks

Picture day is a date, not childcare — school events sit on your calendar as
their day and never count as coverage for a break week.
