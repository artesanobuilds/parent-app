# Camps & school breaks

Camps are the anchor: every school break is a set of weeks that need a plan,
and MVP works the whole problem week by week.

## The flow

1. **Find.** Ask the assistant to go looking ("find art camps near 95448 for
   the weeks we're uncovered") or browse Discovery. You can also snap a photo
   of a flyer in the iPhone app — it extracts the details.
2. **Shortlist.** Save interesting options to the board under Considering.
3. **Decide.** "Help me decide" puts the shortlist side by side — price,
   age-eligibility, which options collide on the same kid and week — and
   records the decision with its reasoning, so future-you knows why.
4. **Register.** The card's Register button opens the signup page. When you
   mark it registered, email watching and the payment/forms to-dos kick in.
5. **Coast.** Confirmations and reminders get picked up; the card moves to
   All set when nothing's left.

## Week coverage

For every break week, each kid is either covered (a camp, a class, a
caregiver) or not — and uncovered weeks are surfaced before they're a crisis.
A blocked-out family week ("we'll be at grandma's") counts as handled.

## Sharing the plan

The plan exports as a spreadsheet (the same workbook parents already keep,
generated instead of hand-maintained), and you can share a read-only link
with a co-parent or caregiver.
