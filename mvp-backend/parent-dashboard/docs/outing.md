# Weekend outings

Sometimes the question isn't "which camp" but "what do we do *this
Saturday*". Outings are the light-weight answer.

## How it works

Ask the assistant something like:

- "What can we do with the kids this weekend?"
- "Ideas for a rainy Saturday"
- "A day trip from the city"

It asks how many ideas you want (a quick 3 or a longer list), searches for
real, current options near you — named places and events with what/where and
a link — and tunes the practicalities to your youngest child: stroller
access, nap-friendly timing, shade, an indoor backup.

## Outings don't clutter the board

Suggestions are **ephemeral by design** — they're a conversation, not a
commitment, so nothing is saved unless you say so. If one's a keeper:

> "Add the tide pools one to our calendar for Saturday."

That creates a calendar entry — and an outing on the calendar never counts as
*camp coverage* for a week; it's a plan for a day, not care for a week.
